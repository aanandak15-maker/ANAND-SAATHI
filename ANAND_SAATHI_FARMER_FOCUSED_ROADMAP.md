---

## 🎯 **FARMER ESSENTIALS - What Farmers Actually Need**

### **🌱 CORE FARMER NEEDS (Priority 1)**

#### **1. Field Mapping & Area Calculation**
- **Simple GPS Walk**: Walk around field to measure area
- **Manual Coordinates**: Enter coordinates if GPS not available
- **Area Display**: Show area in acres/hectares
- **Field Name**: Give field a simple name

#### **2. Crop Information**
- **Crop Selection**: Choose from common crops (Rice, Wheat, Corn, etc.)
- **Planting Date**: When did you plant?
- **Expected Harvest**: When will you harvest?

#### **3. Weather & Alerts**
- **Today's Weather**: Simple weather display
- **Rain Alert**: Will it rain in next 2-3 days?
- **Temperature Alert**: Too hot or too cold for crops?

#### **4. Government Schemes**
- **PM Kisan**: Check if you're registered
- **Punjab Schemes**: Local government benefits
- **Simple Application**: Easy form to apply

#### **5. Basic Financial Tracking**
- **Input Costs**: How much spent on seeds, fertilizer, etc.
- **Expected Income**: How much will you earn?
- **Simple Profit**: Income minus costs
